# Glofox, Xero and un1t.online

The full rules behind the one-line summaries in `CLAUDE.md`. Moved here word for word from `CLAUDE.md` on 19 Nov 2026 (CLAUDEMD.1); keep the two in step when a rule changes.

**Glofox**
- **`POST /2.0/bookings` can 200 with a failure body** (`message_code: YOU_HAVE_NO_CREDITS_LEFT` — live 2026-07-27), so success is HTTP ok AND (no message code and no `success:false`, OR an id) — judge via `interpretBookingResult()` in `src/lib/glofox.js`, never `result.ok` alone and **never id-required** (an id-required rule mislabelled a real booking, live 2026-07-28). **Its success body wraps the booking as `{ success, Booking }`** — captured from prod 2026-09-08, after fourteen months of "the success body carries no harvestable id" (0/9 funnel bookings) which was really a harvest list that didn't know the wrapper. The id inside is `_id` **or** `id`: Glofox spells it `_id` on the `/2.0/bookings` GET item and `id` on the webhook payload, so read both spellings for any booking shape. Every booking path judges this way, the staff Book panel included (`/api/glofox/classes/book`, GLOFOXWRITEJUDGE.1): `YOU_HAVE_BOOKED_FOR_THIS_EVENT` is `alreadyBooked`, a success that made no new booking.
- **`GET /2.0/members/{id}` can 200 with `success:false`** ("Resource not available, empty result cant be processed" — a deleted/merged account, or a trainer id) — judge via `fetchMemberResult()` in `src/lib/glofox.js`, which returns `ok:false, refused:true` for it (judged by `interpretMemberBody()`). Read raw, the error body looks like a member with every field absent: the attendance refresh wrote NULL over 16 contact columns from one (MEMBERRESULT.1, live 30 Aug 2026).
- **`POST /2.0/register` can 200 with `success:false`** (the June 2026 content-type bug's "The first name field is required., …"; `LOGIN_ALREADY_IN_USE,EMAIL_ALREADY_IN_USE` with `message_code: null`, 21 Aug 2026). Its success shape is `{ success: true, user: { _id } }`. `registerGlofoxMember().ok` is judged on the body (`interpretRegisterResult()`: a 2xx, not `success:false`, AND a member id) and a refusal carries `code` + Glofox's words. On `EMAIL_ALREADY_IN_USE`, `findOrCreateGlofoxMember` searches the email once more: one account → linked (no new account, no trial, no password); none / several / a failed search / a failed link write → `needs_review` with the reason. GLOFOXWRITEJUDGE.1.
- **A membership purchase (`POST /2.2/…/plans/{planCode}/purchase`, the trial grant) can 200 with `success:false` or `status:'ERROR'`**, so `purchaseGlofoxMembership().ok` is judged on the body (`interpretPurchaseResult()`; `PENDING-INTENT` is not granted). Approving a `needs_credit_grant` card runs `grantTrialBeforeBooking()` (`src/lib/agent/trial-grant.js`). It buys the card's funnel trial (`details.trial_membership_id`/`trial_plan_code`, stamped by `routeToReview`) or else the location default. A trial that did not take lands the card on `failed` (`TRIAL_GRANT_FAILED` with Glofox's code, `TRIAL_NOT_CONFIGURED`, `GLOFOX_SETTINGS_UNREADABLE`, `TRIAL_GRANT_UNVERIFIED`, `TRIAL_GRANT_UNRECORDED`) with **no booking attempt and no customer message**. The grant is written AHEAD on the row, guarded on the execution's `started_at`: a `{ stage: 'purchasing' }` marker before the purchase (no marker, no purchase) and the outcome after it, before booking. A recorded grant, or credits already on the account, skips the purchase; a purchase whose answer was never recorded (a death mid-approval, or no reply from Glofox) is not bought again unless credits show (`TRIAL_GRANT_UNVERIFIED`, staff check Glofox for the €0 trial invoice). `glofoxFetch` never re-sends the purchase after a 5xx (GLOFOXPOSTRETRY.1): a 5xx is `outcome_unknown`, like no reply. Before TRIALGRANT.1 the result was thrown away: 4 of 16 approvals in 90 days failed `YOU_HAVE_NO_CREDITS_LEFT`, none with a trial behind it. A purchase that went through shows as a €0 `MEMBERSHIPS` `INVOICE_UPDATED` webhook for that member within minutes, which is how to audit one. Since TRIALSTUDIO (24 Oct 2026) the public `/trial` page refuses a Stillorgan buyer with any trial history BEFORE taking money (`src/lib/trial-eligibility.js`: a live `glofox_trial_claims` row, a `trial`/`no_sale_trial` status, a plan named trial, a `created` push event or a granted paid trial; a failed read is 503, never a refusal) and offers "I need help", which files a `help_requested` row on the Paid trials tab; a Hatch Street buyer is sent to un1t.online's hosted checkout (`src/lib/trial-purchase-un1t-checkout.js`) and the row is settled off the `un1t_transactions` mirror by the 2-minute cron (`src/lib/trial-purchase-un1t-settle.js`, one purchase settles at most one row): the CRM never charges a Hatch buyer and never calls `/purchases/charge` or `/credits/grant` for it.
- **A failed Glofox credits/membership read is "unknown", never "no packs"** — `fetchUserCredits()`, which collapsed a failure into `[]`, is gone (TRIALGRANT.1); read `fetchUserCreditsResult()` / `fetchMembershipResult()`. On the shared sync path `buildCreditMemberContext()` flags `creditsFailed`/`membershipsFailed` and `previewMemberSync()` (result carries `credits_unread`): a failed **credits** read keeps the stored `trial_credits_remaining` and a stored `credit_member`; a failed **membership** read still writes the balance and holds only the `credit_member` label. A membership 404 (any 4xx but 429) is an answer, cached for the run; 429/5xx/network are failures, never cached. `glofoxFetch` counts 429/5xx/retries (`glofoxHttpStats()`); the Glofox crons write them as `glofox_http`. The `/start` class-booking processor follows the same rule (CBPCREDITREAD.1): a failed credits read, on the account (unless a sibling's bookable membership, which needs no read, can book), or on a sibling when no readable sibling has a balance, throws `CreditReadError`; the queue retries it, and at the cap files a `credit_check_failed` card (approving it grants nothing). It is never `needs_credit_grant`, whose approve buys the trial membership (`approvalGrantsTrialCredit`), and never `prior_attendance`.
- **`glofoxFetch` never blind-retries a write** (GLOFOXPOSTRETRY.1). A 429 is retried for every method (Glofox throttled it before processing). A 5xx is retried only for `GET`/`HEAD`, or a call that opts in with `retry: 'idempotent'` (the read-POSTs: payments report, member search, payment link, leads filter). Any other method returns its first 5xx unless it passes `retry: { verify }`: after the backoff a dedupe read answers `'landed' | 'absent' | 'unknown'` and only `'absent'` re-sends. Bookings (`findLandedBooking`), booking cancels (`findBookingCancelState`) and registration (email search) verify with ONE-attempt reads, and report a landed first attempt as success (`recovered: 'landed_after_5xx'`); the read also runs after the LAST 5xx, only to report a landing. A booking is "landed" only if created since the send, or an older `BOOKED` one on that event for that member: **an older waitlist entry is `unknown`, never "booked"**. The trial purchase, notes and membership cancel never re-send (a purchase 5xx is `outcome_unknown`; on the `/start` mint path that doubt is stamped on the `needs_credit_grant` card as `details.trial_grant`, so even its first approval books only if credits show). Counters `unsafe_not_retried` and `verify_*` on `glofoxHttpStats()`; one `glofox` warn "write answered 5xx; not retried" per call. `tests/glofox-write-retry-guard.test.js` fails any `glofoxFetch(` call that may be a write unless its `retry:` reads as a policy (`'idempotent'`, `'never'`, `{ verify }`, or a conditional of those; never a variable). A write call (`isGlofoxWrite()`: not GET/HEAD, not `retry: 'idempotent'`, not `readOnly`) that ends 5xx, 429 after retries, aborted, or with no reply leaves ONE `error_events` row, unless its check read found it went through (`route_type 'glofox_write'`, `name glofox_write_5xx|429|no_reply`, id-free `route_path 'glofox:<METHOD> <path>'`), which Sentinel's `crm.error-events` warns on; at most `GLOFOX_WRITE_FAILURE_ROWS_PER_MIN` (5) a minute per instance, so an outage cannot use up the shared 30/min storm guard; the counters stay per instance (GLOFOXWRITEJUDGE.1). A read-POST sent once as a dedupe read passes `readOnly: true`.
- **`glofox_webhook_events.event_id` is the Glofox ENTITY id (`Payload.id`), not the emission** — the emission is `Metadata.trace_id`. A booking's later events (`BOOKING_UPDATED` after the class, `BOOKING_DELETED`) upsert the SAME row, so it holds only the latest event (70% of rows in 30 days, 93% of `BOOKING_UPDATED`). The upsert rewrites `status` to `'received'` and returns it, so the route's `deduped` branch never fires, and **must not be "fixed" with `ignoreDuplicates: true`**: that would silently drop ~57% of deliveries (every later event about a known booking). Per-delivery history (trace, Glofox timestamp, status, a PII-free digest) is `glofox_webhook_attempts` (mig 649, 90-day retention); query it, not `result`, for "which delivery did this". WEBHOOKAUDIT.1.
- **Glofox mints a NEW `invoice_id` per payment *attempt*** (one-off purchases; subscriptions reuse one id) → fail-then-succeed leaves orphans. Same-amount+same-day ≠ dupe.
- **`glofox_invoices` is stale** — never compute "amount owed" from it (only live via `INVOICE_UPDATED` webhooks; PENDING_INTENT frozen).
**Xero**
- **ONE LOCATION = ONE XERO ORGANISATION. Never shared, no exceptions.** Every business in the estate is a separate legal entity (Champ Fitness Ltd, CCF Autos, Givers Consultancy, SourceIt), so a tenant bound to two locations files one company's bills into another company's books. This is standing guidance for **every new location brought online and every new Xero account connected**. It was violated for months because the OAuth callback did `const tenant = tenants[0]` — Xero's `/connections` returns EVERY org the login ever authorised, so "the first one" was the same org on all three connects and nobody was shown a choice: 101 bills / ~€99k landed in the wrong entity. `chooseTenantToBind()` (`src/lib/xero/tenant-binding.js`) now refuses any org another location holds; **Change organisation** on the settings card repoints without re-running OAuth. **Structural since mig 554**: `xero_connections_tenant_id_unique UNIQUE (tenant_id)` — the database itself refuses a second location on the same tenant, so the rule survives a code path that forgets to ask (importer, script, hand-written INSERT). Disconnecting also purges that location's three caches (mig 554 cleaned 509 rows an earlier disconnect had stranded).
- **Changing a location's Xero org invalidates EVERY cached Xero id for it** — `xero_accounts`, `xero_contacts`, `xero_tax_rates` are all per-org. Both `select-tenant` and the callback handle this, but a stale cache surfaces as Xero's opaque *"An existing contact could not be found"* at send time, not as anything pointing at the org. If you ever repoint by another route, re-sync all three.

- **Only Stillorgan is Glofox-connected.** Hatch Street's `branch_id` is a placeholder — nothing to "turn off" there. Integration is fully per-location.
- **A Glofox lookup is per-LOCATION but a contact's email is unique per ORGANISATION** (`contacts_email_org_unique`, mig 712), so a studio-scoped miss is not "nobody": the person may be a sibling studio's contact, and inserting one fails on the index. Resolve the miss through `findGlofoxCrossoverContact` (`src/lib/glofox-crossover.js`) and attach with `recordGlofoxCrossover`, never insert blind (GLOFOXSIBLING.1). The contact is attached, never moved: the link IS its `contact_studios` row at the Glofox studio (source `glofox`; mig 768 dropped the `glofox_crossover_members` table), which is also what makes it readable there. **A write from a non-membership source never weakens that row** (`MEMBERSHIP_SOURCES` in `src/lib/contact-studios.js`: a race or lead-form signup by a Hatch-primary Glofox member used to turn their Stillorgan member row into `lead`/`lead_form`), and the nightly reconcile proves the account by the contact's `glofox_member_id` at a Glofox studio of its organisation, never by the row's own `source` (one Glofox studio per organisation today: a second needs a per-studio id column first). A reader that selects a studio's Glofox-linked contacts scopes through `contactsAtStudio` / `glofoxCrossoverContactIds` + `scopeToGlofoxStudio` (attendance refresh, detail backfill, arrears, invoice CSV import), and person-group code reads `studio_location_ids` via `contactAtGlofoxStudio` (GLOFOXREADERS.1, ORGCONTACTS.2); a bare `.eq('location_id', studio)` on Glofox contacts is the bug. **A sibling-studio contact gets a deal on the Glofox studio's board only when they trained, booked, paid or bought a pack THERE** (`hasActivityAtStudio`, CROSSOVERDEAL.1): the classifier reads the studio's Glofox record, and a race entry or untouched trial classifies as `new_lead`, which put 12 Hatch clients on Stillorgan's cold-call list the night 758 shipped (mig 763 removed them). An existing deal there still moves. **And a person who is an active member or TRAINING at another studio of the org (a live class booking there in the last 60 days or next 30) leaves every derived acquisition board where they are not already a customer** (PIPEEXCL.2, `loadActiveElsewhere` + `leavesBoard`, nightly `pipeline-classify`: status `lost` + a timeline note, never a delete; a failed read holds every create). PIPEEXCL.1 ran on Hatch's board only and knew members only.
- **Hatch Street's membership source is un1t.online, not Glofox** (`membership_source = 'un1t_online'`, registry row `platform='un1t_online'`, cron `un1t-online-sync` every 15 min; connected since #2068). The sync is GET-only, never deletes (the `un1t_*` mirror keeps history the API may stop returning; each run records `counts.history.<table>.absent_from_pull`, so a rolling window shows up there), and at most one run per studio is in flight (mig 735: a second returns `in_progress`). Its token expires 2027-10-16 (warned 30 days out in integration health). Hatch's timetable is projected into `class_occurrences` with `source_platform = 'un1t_online'` (#2078), and its bookings into `class_bookings` with `source_platform = 'un1t_online'` (#2132; explicit `ATTENDED`/`NO_SHOW`, a past un1t `BOOKED` is undecided on the scorecard). The CRM never reminds, rewards or pushes for those rows or at a un1t.online studio (un1t.online sends its own): `src/lib/class-booking-platform.js` (`crmMayActOnClassBooking`, both guards: the row's platform and the studio's `membership_source`) is the rule, pinned by `tests/class-bookings-readers-guard.test.js`. **One exception (OPENWEEK.7, Richard, 19 Nov 2026):** `src/lib/open-week-reminder-send.js` sends a WhatsApp reminder for an open week claimant's booked classes inside the promo window at both studios, Hatch included; it is the only `open-week-messaging` reader the guard allows.
- **`/offers/<studio>` sells through ONE rail rule: `offerRail(location, offer)`** (`src/lib/offer-studios.js`, BLACKFRIDAY.9). At a un1t.online studio a membership or pack is sold only through un1t.online's hosted checkout, and only with `sale_offers.un1t_product_id` set and priced the same as the mirrored product. A row carrying a product id never reaches Revolut. The page and the checkout route both call it. Those purchases are recorded in `offer_purchases` (`payment_provider='un1t_online'`, no Revolut order) only by `sweepUn1tOfferPurchases` (`src/lib/un1t-offer-purchases.js`), an arm of the */2 `process-trial-purchases` cron. It is never part of `un1t-online-sync`, which is write-fenced (UN1TWRITE.7). The sweep reads the `un1t_transactions` mirror, and every in-window purchase of an offer product is recorded once (`UNIQUE (location_id, un1t_transaction_id)`), including ones made in the app or at the desk (`origin='off_page'`). Revolut reconcile, disputes and webhook lookups filter `payment_provider='revolut'`. `offer_purchases` and `sale_offers` are service-role only (mig 827).
- **un1t.online WRITES live only in `src/lib/un1t-online-write.js` (the transport) and `src/lib/un1t-online-write-ops.js` (the primitives)** (UN1TWRITE.1; the sync is fenced from both by `tests/un1t-online-write-guard.test.js`). Every write is judged on the response BODY, never blind-retried, budgeted at 30/min per studio, ledgered in `un1t_write_events` (service-role only) and gated by `locations.settings.un1t_online.writes_enabled`. Never `bypassCredit`/`forceRefund` or a refund: a refund is a record-only `refund_request` card (`fileRefundCard`) done by hand in the owner console. `POST /purchases/charge` is called only from `grantTrial`, for a live €0 product. `POST /credits/grant` appears only in `src/lib/un1t-online-write-ops.js` inside exactly three functions (and the transport's ENDPOINTS allowlist, whose `/credits/grant` entry admits only the ops `trial_regrant`, `promo_grant` and `comp_credits`): `regrantTrialCredits`, unexported and called only from `grantTrial` (TRIALREGRANT.1, Richard's decision 20 Oct 2026): 3 credits for 7 days, once ever per person per studio (`un1t_write_events` op `trial_regrant`, mig 760), only when trial_used is the sole ineligibility, no booking other than a cancelled one, and the live balance is 0 and not unlimited; `grantPromoCredits` (CORPPROMO.1, Richard's decision 28 Oct 2026: the `/corp` corporate promos), called only from `src/lib/corp-promo-claim.js`: the promo's credits and expiry from `src/lib/corp-promos.js` (3 for 7 days at Hatch), once per person per promo (op `promo_grant`, mig 787), only for someone who has never trained at the studio and whose email or mobile never had a free trial there, and only onto a live balance of 0; and `grantCompCredits` (UN1TCOMP.1, Richard's decision 28 Oct 2026: staff complimentary credits from the Hatch card on the contact page), exported and imported and called only by `src/app/api/un1t-online/credits/comp/route.js`: op `comp_credits` (mig 788), policy `never` with the credits-ledger check read, origin `staff` with an actor only, 1 to 10 credits, 1 to 365 days counted on the studio's clock, a reason from the fixed list, gated by the `un1t_comp_credits` web permission at the booking studio (owner, manager and reception by default). Mia never reaches `grantCompCredits` or the `comp_credits` op. Both guards (`tests/un1t-online-write-guard.test.js`, `tests/mia-no-charge-no-refund-guard.test.js`) pin that exact set of functions, each to its own sole caller: a fourth function or another caller fails them. The generated password is never stored, logged or shown. **FEES.1 (Richard's decision 28 Oct 2026): `POST /purchases/charge-custom` is the one paid charge**, inside `chargeClassFee` only (op `fee_charge`, policy `idempotent`, key `fee:<loc>:<feeId>:<attempt>`, the member's default card, never `paymentMethodId`), called only by the hourly `class-fees` cron and the staff `POST /api/class-fees/retry`; never Mia. `charge-custom/link` and card setup requests stay banned (Richard ruled out emailing members a link). A late cancel is un1t.online's own window (`default_cancelation_window_hours`, read live); a pack credit un1t.online kept is only recorded (`class_fees` status `recorded`). FEES.2: automatic forgiveness (swap, new member/trial, first offence on by default; spot refilled, monthly cap off) lives in `src/lib/class-fees-forgiveness.js` and writes the fee as `waived` with `waive_rule` (mig 783); Mia's late cancel quotes it through `src/lib/class-fees-quote.js` (reads only) and cancels only on a second call with `confirm_late_fee: true`. FEES.3: a fee found while charging is paused is `preview` and is NEVER charged (un-pausing charges only fees found afterwards); the member notice email (`fees.notice_enabled`, only while charging is on) and appeals (`fees.appeals_enabled`, public `/fee-appeal/<token>`, only the token's sha256 stored) are independent switches; an appeal holds the fee (`appealed`) until staff waive it or charge it anyway (`src/lib/class-fees-member.js`, mig 784). FEES.4: members on a studio's never-charged list (`class_fee_exemptions`, mig 785, service role only, managed at `/api/class-fees/exemptions`) are never charged or warned by Mia; their offences are written as waived with `waive_rule` `exempt`, adding one waives their open fees there, and the charge runs re-check the list before every claim.
- **A Glofox member's password is never stored** (PASSCODEREAD.1/.2, migs 651/652). `findOrCreateGlofoxMember` registers a new member with a random initial password and returns it ONCE to its caller (the manual Create-in-Glofox button shows it to the staff member who pressed it). `contacts.glofox_passcode` and `glofox_push_events.passcode_sent` were CHECK-constrained NULL (651) and then **dropped** (mig 652, PASSCODEREAD.2; `contact_location_audience` recreated without the column, its privileges kept), so any read or write of them fails (42703 / PGRST204). `{{glofox_passcode}}` always renders empty. `glofox_push_events` holds no client grant: read it through `/api/admin/glofox-push-events`. `tests/glofox-passcode-retired-guard.test.js` enforces all of it.
