## 1. Intent and why

Hatch Street books on un1t.online, and today the CRM can only read it. A website or WhatsApp `/start` lead at Hatch becomes a `manual_booking` card: staff create the un1t.online account and the booking by hand, then approve the card to record it (`class-booking-processor.js:213-217`, `membership-requests/[id]/route.js:433`). Staff cannot book or cancel a Hatch class from the contact page, and Mia cannot either (her booking tools are withheld at Hatch, `tool-capability.js:25-44`).

Richard decided (17 Oct) that all three booking paths at Hatch go through the un1t.online API: Mia, staff, and new people from the website and `/start`. This spec builds the write side those paths share: one write client with Glofox-grade write safety, the local mirror updated the moment a write lands, the staff and new-person paths, the checkout-link primitive spec E's upsell uses, and the conditions for flipping the `bookings` capability.

## 2. Goals

1. A write client for un1t.online: book, cancel, create client, forgot-password, trial grant, checkout link. It never blind-retries a write, judges success on the body, resolves "no clear answer" with a dedupe read, logs every write, leaves an `error_events` row for every failed one, and shares the 60/min budget with the sync.
2. The sync stays read-only by construction: it cannot reach a write.
3. After a write lands, `un1t_bookings` / `un1t_clients` / `un1t_transactions` and the contact link show it immediately, and the next 15-minute sync neither duplicates nor reverts it.
4. Staff at Hatch book and cancel a class from the contact page, dual-studio members included.
5. A new person booking a first Hatch class on the website or `/start` ticks un1t.online's terms and liability waiver, and gets a un1t.online account (random password, never stored or shown, terms recorded as accepted), our explanation email, un1t.online's set-password email, the public "The UN1T Trial" if they are eligible (R1), and the booking, with no staff touch on the happy path and an approval card on every other path.
6. `createCheckoutLink` for spec E: a hosted payment link for a paid product, never a charge. E owns the return page (`/pay-done/<token>`).
7. A written, testable gate for flipping `bookings` to true at Hatch.
8. A live test with a "MIA TEST" account in prod.

## 3. Non-goals

- Mia's conversation behaviour (tool dispatch, upsell, book-after-payment, the offer list, `credits`): spec E. D gives E the primitives.
- Projecting `un1t_bookings` into `class_bookings`, the who's-coming list, fill / no-show, and the hard-off for CRM reminders, attendance pushes and rewards: spec C.
- Pipeline boards (A) and radars / arrears (B).
- A staff "create un1t.online account" button. Accounts are created only on a booking path that also runs the set-password step.
- Creating an account from the plain lead form (`/api/public/leads`): someone who has not booked a class gets no un1t.online account. That route is unchanged.
- `PATCH /bookings/{id}/mark-attendance`, `/purchases/payment-sheet`, `/purchases/charge-custom`, deleting or editing un1t.online users, and `POST /credits/grant`, which nothing in these specs calls (R1d).
- Instagram: Mia is off on Instagram for now (R3), so none of D's primitives is wired to an Instagram turn; Hatch's booking, purchase and upsell paths are WhatsApp, website and staff only.
- A second (write-scoped) token. Richard kept "Repset CRM" (followups P0.2, 17 Oct).
- Bot defences beyond today's public-form validation (`isValidMobileNumber`, PR #1558): no confirmation step and no daily cap. Richard accepts the risk that a bot creates a un1t.online account (G2).
- The checkout return page and any return ping: spec E's `/pay-done/<token>` is the only one.
- Paying through a checkout link in the automated test: Richard tests the paid path by hand after go-live (G4).

## 4. Anti-goals (things this must never do)

- **Never charge a card for a paid product.** `POST /purchases/charge` is called from exactly one function, the trial grant, and only for a product a live read shows at price 0.
- **Never re-send a purchase or a forgot-password**, and never re-send any other write except after a dedupe read answered "absent".
- **Never send `bypassCredit` or `forceRefund`.** Stillorgan's staff book and cancel routes have no "book without a credit" and no "force refund" (`/api/glofox/classes/book/route.js:84-88`, `cancel/route.js:65`), so Hatch gets none either. A comp is a staff action in un1t.online's own console.
- **Never issue a refund (R2).** No code path calls a refund endpoint or sends `forceRefund`. Any refund (a double payment, a cancellation where a refund is wanted, anything else) becomes a staff approval card (`kind 'refund_request'`, `details.provider 'un1t_online'`, with the booking or transaction, the amount if known and the reason); approving it records that staff did the refund in un1t.online and executes nothing. The credit un1t.online itself returns under its own cancel policy is its behaviour, not a refund issued by the CRM. E's double-payment case files this card through `fileRefundCard()`, which D exports.
- **Never store, log, return or show the generated password.** Same rule as Glofox (PASSCODEREAD.1/.2, CLAUDE.md line 65), stricter: not even the caller sees it.
- **Never let the sync call a write**, and never let a write change the sync's run result.
- **Never show capacity to a customer.** Staff surfaces may show spots; public lists and customer copy never do (PUBCAP.1, CAPACITY-SECRECY.1).
- **Never send a CRM class reminder or attendance push for a un1t.online booking** (decision 7; C enforces, D adds nothing that sends one).
- **Never start a new WhatsApp send through another studio's number** (G7, WAHOMESTUDIO.1). D's customer messages are email; the staff chat drop-in replies only inside an existing thread. Org-wide WhatsApp routing is a separate follow-up.
- **Never write another studio's membership or pipeline.** Membership status and the pipeline are separate per location; only the contact is shared (G1). Every mirror write is scoped to the booking studio.
- **Never create an account for someone who has not ticked un1t.online's terms and waiver** (G3).

## 6. Design

### 6.1 Write client: `src/lib/un1t-online-write.js` (new) + `getOne` in `un1t-online.js`

**Claude's call: module split.** `un1t-online.js` gains only reads: `getOne(path)` (one non-paginated GET through the existing `get()`), and exports its error classes and header helpers. Every write lives in a new `un1t-online-write.js` with its own `send(method, path, { body, policy, idempotencyKey })`. The verb pin at `un1t-online.test.js:280` stays as is. A new test fails if any sync-side module (`un1t-online-sync.js`, `-map`, `-contacts`, `-occurrences`, `-studio-preferences`, the cron and Sync-now routes) imports `un1t-online-write`. That is what "read-only by construction" means for the sync from now on.

**Per-call policy (declared at every call site, statically checked like `tests/glofox-write-retry-guard.test.js`):**

| Op | On 5xx / no reply / 2xx with no judgeable body | Dedupe read (one attempt) | Re-send? |
|---|---|---|---|
| book | verify | `GET /users/{id}/bookings`: a `booked`/`attended` row for this event created since the send, or an older `booked` one → landed; only `cancelled` rows → absent; anything else or a failed read → unknown | once, only on absent |
| cancel | verify | `GET /bookings/{id}`: `cancelled` → landed; `booked` → absent; `attended`/`no_show` → refused "already happened" | once, only on absent |
| create client | verify | email walk of `GET /users/clients`: one match → landed (id recovered); none → absent; several or a failed walk → unknown | once, only on absent (a duplicate is refused by email anyway) |
| trial (each of the two calls in 6.6: €0 charge, free checkout) | never | credits ledger read: balance ≥ 1 or unlimited → landed; else unknown | **never** (an unknown step never moves on to the next step) |
| forgot-password | never | none | **never** (a re-send is a second email) |
| checkout | idempotent | none | once, with the **same** `idempotency-key` (no money moves; the key makes it the same session) |

A 429 is retried for every op after the reset wait (un1t.online throttled before processing, as with Glofox); 429 exhausted is "not done", which a queue may retry. 401 / 403 is final: `Un1tAuthError`, permission cache dropped. A landed first attempt reports `recovered: 'landed_after_5xx'`.

**Judging the body** (pure `judgeUn1tWrite(op, { status, body })` → `{ verdict: 'done'|'refused'|'unknown', id, code, message, alreadyBooked }`): done = 2xx AND `success !== false` AND the op's id (`data.id`; `data.transactionId`; `data.checkoutUrl` as an `https:` URL). A 2xx with `success:false` or a 4xx is **refused**, with un1t.online's `error.code` and words. A 2xx with no id is **unknown** and goes to the dedupe read, never "failed" (the Glofox lesson: an id-required rule mislabelled a real booking, CLAUDE.md line 50). Refusal codes are not documented: the MIA TEST run (§10) records the real codes for no credits, class full, already booked, trial already used, email in use and late cancel into one mapping table in the judge. Until then, `alreadyBooked` matches a refusal whose code or message says already booked, and every unmapped code is surfaced verbatim.

**Permissions: fail closed.** Before the first write of an op, `requiredPermissions(op)` is checked against `GET /api-token` (`permissions[]`), cached 5 minutes per token fingerprint (`un1t-online-token.js`), re-read after any 403. The same read checks `studio.id` equals the stored `external_account_id` (as the sync does). Requirements: book `bookings:create` + `bookings:read`; cancel `bookings:update` + `bookings:read`; create `users:create` + `users:read`; trial `purchases:create` + `products:read` + `credits:read`; checkout `purchases:create` + `products:read`; forgot-password none. A missing one returns `UN1T_PERMISSION_MISSING` with nothing sent and this staff-facing text: "The un1t.online token for {studio} is missing the '{permission}' permission, so nothing was sent. Add it to the token in the un1t.online owner console, or issue a new token and paste it in Settings, Integrations, un1t.online." The un1t.online tab shows a "Write permissions" line from the same read (all present / missing list).

**Rate budget shared with the sync.** Each response's headers still govern the instance (existing logic). Across instances, **every HTTP request** the write side makes (writes, dedupe reads, permission and credits reads, the email walk's pages) first takes one slot from `checkRateLimit(db, 'un1t-write:<locationId>', { max: 30, windowMs: 60_000 })` (`src/lib/rate-limit.js:58`); so the write side can never take more than half of un1t.online's 60/min, and the sync's ~9 requests per run fit the rest. **Claude's call** on 30; counting per request, not per operation (D-7), because an operation can be 2 or 10 requests. A spent slot before the write is "not done, try shortly" (the queue retries; a staff route answers 429 with that text). A spent slot between a write and its dedupe read waits for the window (up to 60 s) rather than skipping the read, so a write is never left unjudged for budget reasons.

**Logging and error_events.** Module `un1t-online-write`; fields `{ locationId, op, origin, ledgerId, status, verdict, code, recovered, attempt, waitMs }`. Never the token, an email, a name, a phone, the password or a checkout URL. One `error_events` row per failed write: 5xx / 429 after retries / no reply / unclear 2xx / auth / an unmapped 4xx (`route_type 'un1t_write'`, `name un1t_write_5xx|429|no_reply|unclear|auth|refused`, id-free `route_path 'un1t:<METHOD> <path with :id>'`), skipped when the dedupe read found it landed, capped at 5 a minute per instance with one summary warn (same shape as `glofoxWriteFailureEvent` and `GLOFOX_WRITE_FAILURE_ROWS_PER_MIN`, `glofox.js:588-641`). Known business refusals (no credits, full, already booked, trial used, email in use) are answers: logged and on the ledger, no row. Sentinel's `crm.error-events` check picks the rows up unchanged.

**Switch.** Every primitive refuses `UN1T_WRITES_OFF` unless `locations.settings.un1t_online.writes_enabled === true` (master-only toggle on the un1t.online tab, default off).

### 6.2 The write ledger: `un1t_write_events` (new table)

One row per write attempt; it is the write-ahead marker, the resume point for retries and approvals, the dedupe of our own attempts, and the audit trail.

Columns: `id uuid pk`, `location_id` (fk locations), `contact_id` (fk contacts, on delete set null), `un1t_user_id text`, `op text check in ('create_client','forgot_password','trial','book','cancel','checkout')`, `origin text check in ('staff','funnel','approval','mia','test_account')`, `origin_ref text` (queue row, card or intent id), `actor_user_id uuid` (staff), `idempotency_key text not null`, `target text` (event, booking or product id), `stage text check in ('sent','done','refused','unknown')`, `method text` (trial only: `check in ('charge','free_checkout')`, the step of 6.6 this row is), `un1t_ref text` (created booking / client / transaction id), `terms_accepted_at timestamptz` + `terms_source text` (create only, copied from the request, 6.5), `checkout_url text`, `http_status int`, `code text`, `message text` (≤500 chars, password-scrubbed), `recovered text`, `created_at`, `finished_at`.
Keys: `create:<loc>:<contact>`, `trial:<loc>:<un1tUser>:<product>:<method>`, `book:<loc>:<un1tUser>:<event>`, `cancel:<loc>:<booking>`, `pw:<loc>:<un1tUser>`, and for checkout the caller's idempotency key (E's). That same string is the `idempotency-key` header on the purchase calls.
Partial unique index: at most one `sent`/`unknown` row per key (one attempt in flight or in doubt), and at most one `done` row per key for `create_client`, `trial` and `forgot_password`; plus at most one `done` trial row per `(location_id, un1t_user_id, target)` across the charge and the free checkout, so the CRM can never grant twice. A rebook after a cancel is allowed (a `done` book does not block a new `book` attempt; the next attempt's dedupe read sees the earlier booking).
Index `(location_id, contact_id, op, created_at desc)`.
**Grants (CLAUDE.md lines 16-18, mig 726 pattern):** RLS on, no policy, `REVOKE ALL … FROM anon, authenticated`, service_role only; staff read it through `/api` routes. No column-granted table is touched.

### 6.3 Mirror updates after a write

`applyUn1tWriteToMirror()` in `src/lib/un1t-online-mirror-write.js` (new), called after a `done` (including `recovered`) verdict:

- **book / cancel:** upsert the row through `mapBooking` (the response is the same `BookingResponseDto`; a cancel re-reads `GET /bookings/{id}`) with `contact_id` and `crm_written_at = now()`; then one `GET /credits/ledgers/{userId}/{studioId}` updates `un1t_clients.credit_balance` / `credit_unlimited`; then the `class_bookings` row is upserted exactly as C §7 asks: built with C's `mapUn1tBookingToClassBooking` from the same response row, on `location_id,glofox_booking_id`, carrying the response's `updated_at` (or the request time when it has none). The tie-break is C's (C-4): a terminal status from the sync (ATTENDED, NO_SHOW, CANCELLED) always overwrites a CRM-written BOOKED, and "newer wins" applies only between rows of the same rank; our clock is never compared with un1t.online's for a terminal status. This write needs C's migration (it adds `class_bookings.source_platform`, absent in prod today) to be applied first.
- **create client:** insert the `un1t_clients` row through `mapClient`, and set `contacts.un1t_user_id` on the executing contact only where it is null (`.is('un1t_user_id', null)`). A 23505 on the org unique index (another contact holds that user) is a conflict card, never an overwrite.
- **trial:** when un1t.online returns a transaction id (the charge, or a free checkout that reports one), insert a minimal `un1t_transactions` row (`un1t_id = transactionId`, product id and title from `un1t_products`, `amount_cents 0`, `type 'one_time'`, the product's type, `raw {"source":"crm_write"}`), so A sees "trial" at once; without an id, the `done` trial ledger row stands until the next sync brings the transaction. Both refresh credits.
- **checkout:** nothing (no money has moved).
- **Never** `contact_memberships`: the sync owns it, and a trial is credits, not a subscription.

A failed mirror write never changes the write's outcome: `logError` with ids, and the next sync repairs it.

**Idempotent with the sync (migration).** Add `crm_written_at timestamptz` to `un1t_bookings`, `un1t_clients`, `un1t_transactions`, and one BEFORE UPDATE trigger function `un1t_mirror_keep_crm_write()` on the three: when `OLD.crm_written_at IS NOT NULL AND NEW.synced_at < OLD.crm_written_at AND NEW.crm_written_at IS NOT DISTINCT FROM OLD.crm_written_at` (a sync upsert whose pull began before our write), keep every OLD column except `synced_at`, which takes NEW's. **Rank first, on `un1t_bookings`** (the same rule as C-4): when OLD is `booked` and NEW is terminal (`attended`, `no_show`, `cancelled`), NEW always wins; the time rule applies only between statuses of the same rank. Both timestamps in the time rule are our own clock (run start vs our write), never un1t.online's. The sync never writes `crm_written_at` (it is not in its payload), so its upserts leave it alone; keeping NEW `synced_at` keeps the history-window count (`absent_from_pull`) right. Known residue: a change the member makes in the app between our write and that run's pull waits one more run. Trigger functions need no EXECUTE grant (CLAUDE.md line 17); the function states `REVOKE EXECUTE … FROM PUBLIC, anon, authenticated` anyway. No new client grants. The migration takes the next free number at build time (siblings may take 736+), self-checks the trigger and index against the catalog, and is applied before the code (CLAUDE.md line 23).
**`class_bookings` is C's table:** it does not get this trigger. C's projection ranks first (C-4) and then keeps a row whose stored `raw.un1t.updated_at` is later than the pulled one (C §6 step 3), which is why D stamps `updated_at` as above.

### 6.4 Staff book and cancel from the contact page

**Routes (new, Glofox routes untouched):** `GET /api/un1t-online/classes?location_id=`, `POST /api/un1t-online/classes/book` `{ contact_id, location_id, event_id }`, `POST /api/un1t-online/classes/cancel` `{ contact_id, location_id, booking_id }`. **Claude's call:** separate routes rather than a dispatching `/api/glofox/*`, so Stillorgan's paths stay byte-identical.
**Who:** `getCurrentUser` + `assertLocationAccess(user, location_id)`, where `location_id` is the **booking studio**, not the contact's. The studio's `membership_source` must be `un1t_online`, writes on, and the contact must have `un1t_user_id` with a `un1t_clients` row at that studio (proof the client belongs there), else 400 "This contact has no un1t.online account at {studio}. They can book through the website." Same permission bar as Stillorgan (studio membership, `contact-page-gates.js:175`).
**Classes listed:** `un1t_events` at the studio, `status = 'scheduled'`, starting in the next 14 days, time-sorted, grouped by day like BookPanel; spots left (`capacity - bookings_count`) shown to staff with "as of last sync"; `access_mode 'community'` labelled. un1t.online enforces capacity at booking time.
**Credits shown:** from `un1t_clients`: "Credits: N", "Unlimited", or "No credits: un1t.online will refuse unless they hold a membership", with the as-of time (`crm_written_at` or `synced_at`).
**UI:** `BookPanel` takes a `classProvider` prop (`'glofox' | 'un1t_online'`) that picks the list / book / cancel endpoints; consultations are untouched. Results reuse `classBookResultView` semantics: success, "Already booked in un1t.online" (success), un1t.online's own words on a refusal, and for unknown: "un1t.online did not answer clearly. Check the class in un1t.online before trying again." Undo after booking, plus (new for Hatch) an "Upcoming at {studio}" list from `un1t_bookings ⨝ un1t_events` (booked, future) with Cancel, because the mirror makes that list cheap. **Claude's call** (Stillorgan has Undo only). ### 6.6 The trial grant: eligibility first (R1)

`grantTrial(db, { locationId, un1tUserId, contactId, origin, originRef, actor })` resolves to `{ outcome: 'granted' }` or `{ outcome: 'ineligible', reason }` (`reason` is A's `trial_used` / `membership_history` / `paid_pack`, or `trial_used` when un1t.online refuses), and files a staff card on anything else (`un1t_trial_failed:<code>` / `un1t_trial_unverified`). The eligibility rule is **spec A's**, exported from A's membership-read seam as `trialEligibility(db, locationId, { un1tUserId, contactId })`; D calls it and never re-derives it, so the website, `/start` and Mia (E) apply one rule. The product id comes from `settings.un1t_online.trial_product_id` (a picker of active one-time €0 products on the un1t.online tab, seeded with the public "The UN1T Trial", never the private "1) The UN1T Trial"; unset → `trial_not_configured`).

1. **Eligibility (R1a), before anything is sent:** `trialEligibility(db, locationId, { un1tUserId, contactId })` from spec A. It covers R1a's list: any trial transaction (any trial product) in `un1t_transactions` or a `done`/`sent`/`unknown` `op 'trial'` row on D's ledger (A reads the ledger; D keeps that row shape stable), any past or present membership or subscription (`un1t_subscriptions`, `contact_memberships` `un1t_online_api` / `un1t_online_export`, a recurring-product transaction), and any paid pack purchase. A brand-new account D just created has no history and is trivially eligible. A failed read is never "eligible": the queue retries it, and Mia hands off. The mirror can be up to 15 minutes behind, and un1t.online's own limit (step 3) is the backstop.
2. **Grant:** a live `GET /products` read must show the product active at price 0 (else `trial_product_not_free`, nothing sent). Then the €0 `POST /purchases/charge {userId, productId}`: an admin purchase of the product itself, returning a `transactionId`, so it leaves the same €0 purchase a real trial leaves (the 16 Oct probe saw €0 rows among the 96 transactions) and un1t.online applies its own limit. It is the twin of the €0 Glofox plan purchase Stillorgan makes (TRIALGRANT.1). `grantTrial` is the only caller of `/purchases/charge` (static test). If the charge is refused for any reason other than the limit (for example no saved card): one `POST /purchases/checkout` for the same product with `userId`, done only if it answers `free: true` AND a credits read straight after shows the trial's credits (it completed on the server); a free checkout that still needs the customer counts as refused and its link is never sent.
3. **un1t.online answers "already used / limit"** at either call → `ineligible` (path R1b: told, then offered the options). **Any other failure** → staff card `un1t_trial_failed:<code>`. **Unknown** (5xx, no reply, unclear body) → resolved only by the credits read; if credits do not show, card `un1t_trial_unverified`, and the free checkout is never tried after an unknown charge.

Each call is a ledger row (`op 'trial'`, its `method`), judged and never re-sent. The `idempotency-key` header is that row's key, so even a human retry cannot double-buy if un1t.online honours it. A prior `sent`/`unknown` trial row makes the person ineligible for a new attempt and books only if credits show. `POST /credits/grant` is not used, for trials or anything else (R1d).
