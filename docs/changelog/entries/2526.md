| #2526 | BOOKQUEUE.1 — class bookings reach Glofox one at a time | 2026-11-21. Every class booking is now sent to Glofox one after another through a `class-bookings` QStash queue (parallelism 1), instead of all at once. An Open Week claim that booked five classes together had two refused as "no credits left" while the account held credits; staff then saw cards asking them to add a credit that was not needed. A guard test keeps every publish on the queue. No migration. |
