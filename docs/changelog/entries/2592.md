| #2592 | AUTOSAVE.1 — campaign drafts save themselves | 2026-11-26. A campaign draft that has been saved once is now saved again every minute when anything on it has changed, so closing the tab or losing the connection no longer loses the work since the last click on Save. Scheduled campaigns are never saved automatically, because that would change what goes out at the scheduled time, and a campaign that was never saved is not created by autosave. |
