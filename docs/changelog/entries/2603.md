| #2603 | MACHINEOPEN.1 — opens by Apple's mail servers no longer count | 2026-11-26. About 58% of recorded email opens were Apple Mail's servers fetching the email on arrival, not people reading it. From now on those fetches are not counted: they no longer raise open rates, A/B results or a contact's "last opened", and they no longer count as engagement for automations that look at opens. They still prove the email was delivered. Opens recorded before today are unchanged, and opens through Gmail and Yahoo still count. |
