| #2236 | CARDLENS-M.B — phone contact screen acts at the chosen studio (OTA) | 2026-10-27. The staff phone's contact screen gets a studio switcher (one pill per studio the viewer can act at for the person, plus All studios, view only), the twin of the web ActingStudioBar. `GET /api/contacts/[id]/command-centre?scope=drawer` takes `studio=` and judges WhatsApp, email, notes and the new `task` flag, the WhatsApp window and templates at the acting studio (kudos and the cancellation form stay at the primary); `GET /api/contacts/[id]/messaging` takes `location_id` through `resolveRouteStudio`. WhatsApp, company email, Mail and notes are sent for the acting studio, and a Task button opens New task for the contact at that studio, saved through `POST /api/activities/tasks`. Without the `studio` param (the pipeline drawer, older phones) everything stays at the primary. |
