A Meta-ads product page for Stillorgan (its own ad account) selling a paid trial (€39, 14 days by default). A failure after the money is taken is `needs_review` + an ops alert, never silent. Carries the Hatch ads-page learnings (price panel, lead at the POST, trial first, attribution, Lead + Purchase to the studio's dataset). Needs the Glofox plan picked in the block, mig 762 applied and the block on the Stillorgan row before it sells. |
