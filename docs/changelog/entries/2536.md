| #2536 | INVOICEPREP.2 — large invoice emails arrive through the inbound shim | 2026-11-22. An invoice email with more than about 3.3 MB of attachments never reached the CRM (Vercel rejects the body before the route runs). The Supabase Edge Function `postmark-inbound-shim` gains a second target for the invoices webhook: it stages attachments in `inbound-invoices` and forwards a small body, and the invoices ingest reads the staged files and removes them once every attachment is stored. An attachment that was not received (over 25 MB, more than 25 files, or a failed move) gets a visible row. The support target is unchanged. Not live until the cutover in the plan (set the function secret, redeploy with JWT verification off, repoint Postmark). No migration. |
