| #2598 | SEGGROUPS.1 — audience filters can hold one level of groups | 2026-11-26. Engine side of filter groups: an audience can now be "members AND (lapsed 30 days OR never attended)", with each group carrying its own AND or OR, one level deep. Campaigns, automations, segments and WhatsApp sends all read it the same way, and a campaign's detail page shows a group as (a OR b). Tag, event, studio-list, campaign and visit filters stay on their own line, not inside a group. The builder buttons to make groups come in the next change. |
