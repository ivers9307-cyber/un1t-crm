| #2496 | RAFFLE.1 — digital raffle for charity events | 2026-11-20. A Raffle page on any charity event (/events/[id]/raffle): staff sell tickets at a till (search name, email or phone among the event studio's contacts, or add a guest with name and email; quantity; cash or card), the buyer is emailed their numbers, and the draw picks a ticket at random per prize with a full-screen view and a winners list. Managers set the raffle up, void till mistakes (not once a ticket is drawn) and mark a draw not claimed. The raffle never creates or changes a contact. Mig 864 (raffles, raffle_sales, raffle_tickets, raffle_draws, sell_raffle_tickets; service-role only) applied before deploy. |
