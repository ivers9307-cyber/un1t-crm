A race event with the Find me a team option on shows it first among the team sizes on the public signup; choosing it books and charges one person as a team of one flagged `looking_for_team` (mig 766: the flag, `placed_into_registration_id`/`placed_at`/`placed_by`, the `placed` status, `race_events.find_team` copy), taking no team slot in teams capacity mode and one person in people mode. Solo teams are per entrant (no shared team row by name), so a namesake can never resume or delete another entrant's booking. |
