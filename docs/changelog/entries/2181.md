| #2181 | PAIDTRIAL.3 — a paid trial creates the Glofox account despite a phone-only match | 2026-10-21. The resolver's step 2.5 stopped the mint when another Glofox account already held the buyer's mobile under a different email (the free-trial rule: a second account is a second free trial). A paid trial has nothing to double up and Glofox allows one mobile on several accounts, so `findOrCreateGlofoxMember` gains `phoneMatchPolicy` (`'review'` default, callers unchanged; `'create'` mints on the buyer's email and returns the match as `phone_match`, never a link) and the paid-trial processor passes `'create'`, grants, and leaves a system note on the contact naming the other Glofox id(s) for a later merge.
