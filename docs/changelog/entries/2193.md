| #2193 | FINDTEAM.9 — placed rows: targets cannot be cancelled or stale-deleted; solo team rows do not accumulate | 2026-10-24. Closes the two residuals the Find me a team review parked. A team entry that solos were placed into can no longer be cancelled (`POST /api/registrations/[id]/cancel` answers 409 `has_placed_solos`, the same body as the registration DELETE, from `cancelRaceRegistration`, so Mia's cancel and the approvals route inherit it) or stale-deleted by a captain's re-book (409 `already_registered` whatever its own status reads), and a placed row whose target is gone is refused at check-in instead of checking in the dead solo row. A refused or abandoned solo attempt (members-only, bad promo, a failed registration insert, a payment that would not start, a cleared stale pending row) now discards the team row it made, once a count shows no registration points at it, so failed attempts never use up the 20 name suffixes; a paid or placed solo's team is never touched. No migration. |
