| #2609 | CONDBLOCKS.2 — Claude can write conditional email text | 2026-11-26. The email tools Claude uses (create and update a draft, a draft from a template, the resend subject) now accept the conditional blocks from #2606: {{#if member}}…{{else}}…{{/if}}, {{#unless …}}, first_name and stage=<slug>. A block that is badly written, uses an unknown condition, is nested, or opens in one paragraph and closes in another is refused with a plain explanation instead of reaching an email as literal braces. Draft subjects are now checked for unknown tags as well. |
