| #2583 | UNSUBPATH.1 — the consent log records how an unsubscribe arrived | 2026-11-26. Every email opt-out through the unsubscribe link now records which way it came (the mail provider's one-click header, the page opening, or a button press on the page) and the browser that sent it. Until now all three looked the same. In the last 90 days 79 of 263 such opt-outs landed within two minutes of the email going out, and those people later resubscribed about eight times as often as the rest, which looks like mail security scanners opening the page. This change only records the evidence; what is applied is unchanged. |
