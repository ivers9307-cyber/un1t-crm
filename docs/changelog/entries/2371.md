| #2371 | TRIALABANDON.1 — an unpaid €39 trial checkout can start a follow-up sequence, and paying ends it | 2026-11-14. Most people who start the €39 trial checkout on `/trial` never pay, and nothing followed them up. New sequence trigger `paid_trial_checkout_abandoned` ("€39 trial: checkout started, not paid"), for any studio; it does nothing until an operator activates a sequence on it. `sweepAbandonedTrialCheckouts` (`src/lib/trial-checkout-abandoned.js`), a new arm of the */2 `process-trial-purchases` cron with its own heartbeat `trial-checkout-abandoned-sweep`, first exits anyone in such a sequence who has since paid at that studio (`exit_reason 'trial_purchased'`: a paid `trial_purchases` row, or at a un1t.online studio a paid-trial transaction in the mirror), then offers each unjudged row of the last 72 hours that is `payment_failed`, `expired`, `failed` with `checkout_not_opened`, or `awaiting_payment` for 60 minutes, once per person per studio. Left out: a purchase or a row staff hold at or after it, a follow-up in the last 14 days, someone mid-checkout (judged later), a trial history at a Glofox studio, an active member or mirror purchase at a un1t.online one. A failed read holds that person and the heartbeat; every other outcome stamps `trial_purchases.abandon_followup_at` / `abandon_followup_outcome` after the enrolment. The step senders re-check for a purchase right before each send (fails open). Copy can use `{{trial_resume_url}}` in email and the WhatsApp variables `trial_resume_url` / `trial_resume_suffix`: `/trial?studio=<key>` on the studio's customer host, with `&purchase=<id>` only while the Revolut checkout can still be handed back, and `utm_source=crm`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign=trial-abandoned`. Marketing consent gates every step as for any sequence. Migrations: 835 (the two columns) BEFORE the deploy, 836 (the heartbeat row) right AFTER. |
