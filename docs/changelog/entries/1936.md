| #1936 | AC-RETRY.1 — retry a transient AC auto-off failure at the next tick; alert only after three misses (mig 704) | 2026-10-03. Sensibo's own command history showed every failed gym-floor OFF as Failed/Timeout: the request reached Sensibo and the pod did not acknowledge; ONs never fail and the pod recovers within minutes. The ops alert waits for the 3rd consecutive transient miss, fires on the 1st persistent one, and repeats at most hourly. mig 704 adds `ac_sessions.auto_off_attempts` and `auto_off_alerted_at` (cron-written only).
