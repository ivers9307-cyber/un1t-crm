| #2542 | INVOICEPREP.3 — supplier invoices are read and checked on arrival | 2026-11-22. Every row arriving in `invoices_queue` (email, upload, expenses, contractor, car documents, coverage upload) is queued with `queueInvoicePreparation` and read by the `invoice-analysis` queue. `prepareInvoiceRow` reads the file, checks for a duplicate file, a duplicate supplier and number and a bill already in Xero, fills the account and VAT rate from supplier memory, and writes `extracted` with `review_reasons` (13 reasons; empty means ready). An unreviewed statement or other non-invoice becomes the new status `not_invoice` and can be restored; a row a person approved is flagged, never set aside. Bulk send skips any row with a reason, and Approve + send refuses a credit note. Nothing is sent to Xero without a click. The inbox shows reason chips, a Reading state, a Not an invoice filter and a link to the earlier copy of a duplicate. Migration 872 (applied): `review_reasons`, `prepared_at`, `duplicate_of_id`, the `not_invoice` status, `xero_supplier_defaults.default_tax_type`, a seven-argument `upsert_supplier_default`, and `claim_invoice_analysis_batch` claiming `received` rows. The existing backlog is not read until its rows are stamped. |
