| #2203 | PINSCOPE.1 — studio PIN login matches only people at the device's studio; signup trigger ignores the metadata role | 2026-10-25. Two security findings from the SaaS re-audit. (1) `findProfileByPin` now requires the device's `locationId` and matches only members there, org admins of its organisation and the master (`pinHoldersAtLocation`); a shared PIN is refused (`PinAmbiguousError` → 403, counts to the lockout); `set-pin` uniqueness is per organisation (`pinHoldersInOrgs`) and POST is rate-limited 10/15 min per user. Before, a PIN matched anyone in the estate, so staff at one org could sign in as another org's staffer on their own paired device. (2) Mig 772: `handle_new_user` mints an INACTIVE `staff` profile with no location instead of trusting `raw_user_meta_data.role` (a public sign-up could have minted a master); `/api/staff` sets `active: true` with the role. Apply mig 772 after the deploy. |
