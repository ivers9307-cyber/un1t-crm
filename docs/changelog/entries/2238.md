| #2238 | PAIDTRIALONLY.2 — the WhatsApp booking Flow sends new people the paid trial | 2026-10-28. New Mia setting "Booking form paid trial reply (WhatsApp)" (`flow_paid_trial_text`), set per studio. When it is set, someone new who books through the WhatsApp booking Flow is not booked and gets no account or free trial: they are sent the reply (the /trial link) and the queue row closes `failed` / `paid_trial_offered`. Glofox: at the mint point and in place of `needs_credit_grant`. un1t.online: in place of `grantTrial`, on the funnel only. An unreadable setting throws (retry), never a free trial. Unset = the old behaviour. The template editor can now make data-exchange Flow buttons (no entry screen), the kind the live booking Flows use. No migration. |
