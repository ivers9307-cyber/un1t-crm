| #2545 | MIAPAGES.1 — Mia reads the live pages and offers | 2026-11-23. Mia has a new read-only tool, `list_current_offers` (`src/lib/agent/offer-tools.js`), in all five tool blocks on WhatsApp and Instagram. It lists every live landing page and every sellable `/offers` storefront offer in the conversation's organisation, per studio, with the price, end date and link, read live through `readCurrentOffers` (`offer-read.js`) with the pages' own "can this be sold now" checks (the trial entry, the promo row, the Open Week window, the storefront's rules, stock). Pages are ranked (`rankPages`): an offer with an end date leads while it runs, then the open-ended offers with the paid trial first; a page's "Mia's priority" (Automatic, Lead with this, Only if asked) overrides that, and "Who is this for? (Mia reads this)" tells her who to suggest it to. Both are new optional fields in the page editor, stored in `landing_pages.chrome` (`mia_priority`, `mia_note`). Mia's rules (`CURRENT_OFFERS_RULES`) make the tool her only source for an offer's price, end date and link: she works out what the customer wants, offers the lead entry that fits and sends the page link on a yes. A failed read is "unreadable", never "no offers"; nothing internal is in the result. No migration. Not yet tried with a real message. |
