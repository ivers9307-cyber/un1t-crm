| #2163 | LIEU.1 — time-in-lieu ledger for Sunday and public-holiday work (FTE only) | 2026-10-20. Organisation of Working Time Act s.14/s.21 record for the WRC inspection of 21 Oct. Rule (Richard): an FTE employee who works a Sunday banks shift hours × the studio's Sunday multiplier (`locations.settings.payroll.sunday_multiplier`, default 4/3, "time and a third") as TIME IN LIEU, not a pay premium; a public holiday worked (`bank-holidays.js`, the studio's country) banks base time; both on one day earn the greater once; contractors earn nothing. Mig 760 creates `staff_lieu_credits` (hours, signed; `shift_assignment_id` UNIQUE so a shift is credited once; RLS on, no policy, no client privilege) and the `lieu-credits` heartbeat row. `GET /api/cron/lieu-credits` (nightly 03:40 UTC; `?days=N` ≤ 400 backfills) runs `runLieuCreditSweep` (`src/lib/lieu-credits.js`, rule pure in `src/lib/lieu-rules.js`) over every completed, non-cancelled FTE shift on a Sunday / public holiday in the window, upsert-ignore-duplicates on the unique index, stamping only when the estate was read. `GET /api/schedule/allowances` carries `lieu { year, credited_hours, taken_hours, balance_hours }` (omitted when unreadable, absent for contractors; `profileScope` moved to `src/lib/allowance-scope.js`); `GET/POST/DELETE /api/schedule/allowances/lieu` is the ledger (master/owner/manager AT the studio records lieu taken, refused past the balance, or a manual credit with a note; automatic rows cannot be removed). `GET/PUT /api/locations/[id]/payroll` + `PayrollLieuCard` (owner/master) set the multiplier through `mergeLocationSettings`. `staff_cost` gains `sunday_hours`, `public_holiday_hours`, `lieu_hours_earned` and totals (pay unchanged); `week-cost` gains `sunday_hours` (hours only); the WRC pack gains `Lieu hours credited / taken / balance` on the staff CSV and `Public holiday` + `Lieu hours earned` on the shift CSV. Web `AllowanceSummary` and the phone's leave form show a Lieu line in hours. Tested on mocked builders only (no live DB in the session): the upsert against the unique index and the embed at estate volume are unverified. |
