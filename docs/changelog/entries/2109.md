| #2109 | WAORGNUMBER.5 — a WhatsApp reply lands with the studio that spoke last | 2026-10-18. New `routeInboundThread` (`src/lib/whatsapp-inbound-route.js`, spec R4) decides an inbound message's studio on an organisation number: the quoted message's thread, a click-to-WhatsApp ad's studio, the member's thread with the latest non-failed outbound in 7 days, their home studio, else the number's home; transitionally (until WAORGNUMBER.6) a number-less home studio or a thread moved to one (`numberless_home`). The webhook and the coexistence ingest use it (the R2 home choice, `actingLocationId` and `findMovedNumberlessThread` are gone; consent, Flows, Mia, push and the CTWA Lead act at the routed studio, a `numberless_home` Lead still goes out for the receiving number's studio), and stamp the receiving number when it is switched on there. The studio picker lists studios the receiving number is switched on at. `getOrCreateConversation` (R3) stamps new threads with the sending studio's number and replaces a stamp no longer switched on there. No migration. |
