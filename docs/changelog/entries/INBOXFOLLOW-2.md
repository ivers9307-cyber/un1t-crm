| #INBOXFOLLOW-2 | INBOXFOLLOW.2 — snooze a thread, and "Handled by" says who has it | 2026-11-25. A coach can now snooze a WhatsApp or Instagram thread from its header: pick a preset or a time on the studio's own clock and add a note. The thread moves to Waiting and comes back to Needs reply at that time, or at once if the customer writes. A snoozed "Mia needs help" thread waits with the rest until the snooze ends. The "Handled by" control keeps its Mia and You buttons and now says underneath who has the thread: the coach's first name (the person it is assigned to, otherwise whoever sent the latest staff message; on Instagram the assigned person only), "A coach" when no name is known, "Mia paused" on WhatsApp, or "Waiting for a person" when Mia has asked for help and nobody has replied. A WhatsApp message sent by staff from a contact's page, a payment link or a cancellation form now also takes the thread out of Mia needs help, since a person has acted. The inbox list still loads when the approval badges cannot be read. |
