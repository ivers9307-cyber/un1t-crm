| #INBOXFOLLOW-2 | INBOXFOLLOW.2 — snooze a thread, and "Handled by" says who has it | 2026-11-25. A coach can now snooze a WhatsApp or Instagram thread from its header: pick a preset or a time on the studio's own clock and add a note. A snoozed thread sits in Waiting until its time, or comes straight back if the customer writes. When the time comes it returns to Needs reply marked "Snooze ended" with the note, unless a coach is still inside the studio's quiet window on a thread they replied in, in which case it stays in Waiting until that window runs out. A snoozed "Mia needs help" thread waits with the rest until the snooze ends. Mia's follow-ups, first-class check-ins, the automatic resolve and the 60-minute "needs a human" alert all leave a snoozed thread alone. If a customer message arrives just before a coach clicks Snooze, the snooze is refused and the coach is asked to read it first. The "Handled by" control keeps its Mia and You buttons and now says underneath who has the thread: the coach's first name (the person it is assigned to, otherwise whoever sent the latest staff message; on Instagram the assigned person only), "A coach" when no name is known, "Mia paused" on WhatsApp, or "Waiting for a person" when Mia has asked for help and nobody has replied. A WhatsApp message sent by staff from a contact's page, a payment link or a cancellation form now also takes the thread out of Mia needs help, since a person has acted. The inbox list still loads when the approval badges cannot be read. |
