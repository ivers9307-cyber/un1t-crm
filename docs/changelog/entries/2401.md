| #2401 | STALEWATCH.1 — an in-CRM stale-job watchdog with acknowledge and a daily digest | 2026-11-16. New `/api/cron/stale-job-watchdog` (every 15 min, `src/lib/stale-job-watchdog.js`) alerts the masters once per job that newly goes stale (plus an `error_events` row for Sentinel), clears it on recovery and sends one daily digest from 08:00 Dublin. A master can acknowledge a known fault for 7 days from a Background jobs card on `/admin/health`; acknowledged jobs stop alerting and no longer hold `/api/cron/health-check` red. Mig 843 (apply before deploy) adds the acknowledge/alert columns to `cron_heartbeats` and `cron_health`; mig 844 (apply after deploy) seeds the watchdog heartbeat. |
