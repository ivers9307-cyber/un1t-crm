| #2596 | CAMPFILTER.1 — audiences by who opened or clicked a campaign | 2026-11-26. The audience builder has two new filters under Email behaviour: "Clicked a campaign" and "Opened a campaign", each with a list of this studio's sent campaigns. They make follow-ups possible such as "people who clicked the Black Friday email but did not buy" or "everyone who did not open last week's email". The opened filter says that mail apps record opens for people who never read the email, so clicks are the firmer signal. Only campaigns from the same studio can be chosen. |
