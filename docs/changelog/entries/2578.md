| #2578 | INBOXPERM.1 — Instagram conversations need the inbox permission at their own studio, and the inbox stops reloading in bursts | 2026-11-25. The Instagram conversation list and every action on a thread (open, resolve, reply, snooze, pause or resume Mia, assign, link to a contact, add as a contact) now ask for the inbox permission at the conversation's own studio, as the WhatsApp inbox does. Before, someone who held the permission at one studio and was only a member of another could read, reply to and resolve the other studio's Instagram threads whenever the studio they had selected was the one where they held it. A studio they do not belong to still answers "not found". The list without a studio named covers only the studios where the permission is held. Instagram conversations and messages read directly by the browser (live updates) are likewise limited to the studios where the inbox permission is held; someone with only the web inbox permission and not the phone one gets Instagram updates on the one-minute refresh instead of live, as for WhatsApp. Instagram and WhatsApp media, and the WhatsApp conversation list, now need the inbox permission at the studio they belong to (the list without a studio named covers only the studios where it is held). The thread header wraps, so Resolve and Snooze are always reachable even with the contact panel open. A refresh that gets stuck (a laptop that slept, a network switch) no longer freezes the inbox: the list request times out after 20 seconds and the next refresh runs. The unified inbox also no longer reloads its list several times for one message: a burst of live updates becomes one reload (after a 0.4 second pause, never more than 2 seconds late), and only one list request runs at a time. Refresh, the one-minute poll and the actions on a thread still reload at once. |
