| #INBOXFOLLOW-3 | INBOXFOLLOW.3 — Mia can nudge a thread your team took over, once, if you switch it on | 2026-11-25. Each studio can now set how long a thread a coach replied in may stay quiet before it comes back to Needs reply (4 hours to 14 days, 48 hours unless changed), and can switch on one nudge from Mia at that point. The nudge is off by default. Outside WhatsApp's 24-hour window it goes as an approved template the studio picks, and only to customers with marketing consent; inside the window Mia sends a short message of her own. With no template chosen, Mia skips the nudge and the thread simply comes back to your team. If the customer replies, the reply goes to your team and Mia stays silent. The nudge is never sent on a thread that is snoozed, completed, paused, blocked or opted out, never about a pause or a cancellation, only in daytime hours, and it counts inside the same daily limit as Mia's other follow-ups. The quiet period, the switch and the template are under Follow-ups, "Threads your team is waiting on"; an organisation can share the quiet period but never the switch or the template. The inbox lists now read the follow-up settings while they load the conversations. |
