| #2246 | UN1TMOVE.1/.2 — move a un1t.online class booking in one call | 2026-10-28. Staff (a Move button on the contact page's un1t card) and Mia (move_class_booking, or an approval card in draft mode) now reschedule a Hatch class with un1t.online's POST /bookings/{id}/move instead of cancel then book, so the credit and spot are never lost in between. Judged on the body, settled by a check read after a 5xx, never re-sent blind, ledgered as op move. Inside the late-cancel window a move is allowed only to a class the same studio day, and a booking moved there can never be cancelled by the CRM afterwards (un1t_write_events.late_window). A new operator-editable "your booking was moved" message goes after staff and approved moves. Mig 786 (applied before merge). |
