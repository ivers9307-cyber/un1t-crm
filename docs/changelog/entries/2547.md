| #2547 | VATRATE.1 — a supplier bill takes the purchases VAT rate | 2026-11-23. Each studio's Xero holds a sales and a purchases rate at the same percentage, both usable on expenses, so the rate derived from a bill's figures was ambiguous and 54 of 87 read invoices were flagged "No VAT rate". `resolveBillTaxType` now picks the one rate named as a purchases rate when every other match is a sales rate (by name, or Xero's `OUTPUT` type); two purchases rates, none, or any other kind of rate beside it stay ambiguous. `prepare.js` loads the rate name. The sweeper's re-check now also covers rows flagged "No VAT rate" and treats a tax-rate refresh as a change. A person's tax type is never replaced. No migration. |
