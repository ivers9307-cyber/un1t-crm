At a un1t.online studio the hourly class-fees cron lists the day's no-shows and late cancels (un1t.online's own window) at 23:00, emails the review contacts a link to /class-fees (Money hub), and at 17:00 the next day charges each fee not waived to the member's default card through un1t.online "Charge custom amount", described by the class, day and time. A refused charge is retried 7 days later, then shows as outstanding on the member's profile with Retry charge and Waive. Pack credits un1t.online kept are recorded only. chargeClassFee is the one paid charge (cron + staff retry only, never Mia).
