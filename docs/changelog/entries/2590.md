| #2590 | HATCHVISIT.1 — audiences can filter on un1t.online visits | 2026-11-26. Hatch Street's visits are recorded by un1t.online, not on the contact record, so no audience filter could find Hatch members who had or had not been in lately. A new filter, "Visited here in the last (un1t.online)", takes 30, 60, 90, 180 or 365 days: "is" keeps people who came in at this studio within that window, "is not" keeps everyone else, including people who never came. It works in campaigns, segments and automations like the other studio filters. |
