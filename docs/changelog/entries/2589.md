| #2589 | EMAILUTM.1 — links in marketing email say they came from email | 2026-11-26. Links in campaigns and automation emails that point back at the studio's own site now carry utm_source=email and utm_medium=campaign or automation, so a visit or a new lead from an email is recorded as coming from email. Only those two tags are added: the site treats the other tracking tags as a sign of a Meta ad, and email readers must not be counted as ad leads. Links to other sites, the unsubscribe and preference pages, and links that already have a source tag are left alone. |
