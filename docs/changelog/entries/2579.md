| #2579 | ADDCONTACTBLANK.1 — adding a contact from a thread works without an email | 2026-11-26. In the WhatsApp inbox, "Add to Contacts" on a thread was refused whenever the email box was left empty, although the form marks it optional: the empty box was sent as an empty address and failed the email check. An empty email or phone now counts as not given, on WhatsApp and Instagram. When the form is refused for a real reason (a mistyped email, say) the reason now shows inside the form in plain words, where before it was a browser pop-up. |
