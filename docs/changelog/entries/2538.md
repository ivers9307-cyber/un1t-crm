| #2538 | USUALCOACH.1 — usual coaches per shift template | 2026-11-22. A shift template now remembers its usual coaches. Managers tick them in the template editor; the nightly roster job puts them on the shifts it creates, and a new "Fill usual coaches" button on an empty week fills the shifts that already exist. A coach who has left, is on approved leave, has said they can't work that day, or is already on an overlapping shift is skipped, and a shift that is already staffed, on a published roster or full is left alone; the result says how many were added and why any were skipped.
