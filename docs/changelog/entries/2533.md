| #2533 | HOSTSTUDIONOTICE.1 — staff are told when a published host event starts using a studio | A host's edit to a published event that links it to a studio, moves it to another studio, or makes it use its studio for longer (a session earlier than the first or later than the last, or a longer hold) now sends the org's admins a push and an email saying what changed, e.g. "linked to Hatch Street" or "now runs later (last session 21:00) at Hatch Street". It is a notice only: the save is never blocked or changed, nothing needs approval, and a failed notice is logged without failing the save. Moving out of a studio, a shorter duration, a session inside the current hours, drafts and cosmetic edits send nothing. No migration. |
