| #1940 | WANUMBERLESS.1 — inbound WhatsApp threads for a studio with no number are filed at the receiving number's studio | 2026-10-04. Seen live on Hatch Street's opening weekend: two broadcasts went from the Stillorgan number to Hatch cohorts and the replies landed in the Hatch inbox behind "No WhatsApp number is connected at this location." The webhook filed every thread at the contact's home studio, and a number-less studio is refused by the reply path on purpose (WACONFIGFALLBACK.1) and never gets the receiving-number stamp (WAREPLYNUMBER.1), so the thread could be read but not answered while its outbound half sat in a Stillorgan thread. `handleIncomingMessage` now checks `getLocationWhatsAppNumberConfig` for the contact's studio when it differs from the receiving number's; no active row (or a failed lookup, logged) files the thread at the receiving studio, where the stamp applies and replies work. A studio with its own number keeps its threads exactly as before. Tests: `route.reply-number.test.js` (three cases) + the exact caller table in `tests/whatsapp-config-callers.test.js`. The two stray 4 Oct threads were merged into their Stillorgan twins by SQL. |
