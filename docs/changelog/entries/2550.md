| #2550 | INVHARDEN.1 — invoice reader: safety net, outage retries, keep a good reading | 2026-11-23. The `process-invoice-analysis` sweeper now runs `queueMissedInvoices`: `received` rows older than ten minutes that were never queued or prepared are queued (20 a tick) with a logged warning. On the queue, a reader outage (timeout, dropped connection, 429, 5xx) leaves the row queued and claimed with a note in `extraction_error` and is retried when the claim goes stale; the third failure marks it unreadable, and a read a person asked for reports at once. Reading an already-read row again keeps the earlier reading when the new read fails or throws. "Extract selected" queues every chosen row through `bulk-queue-analysis` instead of reading the first ten inside the request. No migration. |
