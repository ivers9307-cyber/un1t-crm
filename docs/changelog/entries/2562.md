| #2562 | CLAIMNAME.1 — fill a matched contact's missing surname on promo and trial claims | 2026-11-23. A promo claim or paid trial purchase stamped the typed names on a matched contact only when its first name was null, so an older contact with a first name and no surname kept the blank surname and the Glofox register refused it (the claim landed on Approvals as Credits not added). `fillMissingContactNames()` now fills each blank name on its own and never replaces one the contact has. No migration. |
