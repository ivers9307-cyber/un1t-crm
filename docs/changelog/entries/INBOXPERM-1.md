| #XXXX | INBOXPERM.1 — Instagram conversations need the inbox permission at their own studio, and the inbox stops reloading in bursts | 2026-11-25. The Instagram conversation list and every action on a thread (open, resolve, reply, snooze, pause or resume Mia, assign, link to a contact, add as a contact) now ask for the inbox permission at the conversation's own studio, as the WhatsApp inbox does. Before, someone who held the permission at one studio and was only a member of another could read, reply to and resolve the other studio's Instagram threads whenever the studio they had selected was the one where they held it. A studio they do not belong to still answers "not found". The list without a studio named covers only the studios where the permission is held. The unified inbox also no longer reloads its list several times for one message: a burst of live updates becomes one reload (after a 0.4 second pause, never more than 2 seconds late), and only one list request runs at a time. Refresh, the one-minute poll and the actions on a thread still reload at once. |
