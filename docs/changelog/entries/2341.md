| #2341 | FINDTEAMDEFAULT.1 — a race that offers Find me a team opens with it selected | 2026-11-09. A race's size selector opened on the smallest team size, so the signup form started with a team name and teammate rows even where most entrants come alone (Richard, for the 21 Nov charity day at Stillorgan). When the event has the find-team option on (race kind), the load effect in `RaceSignupWidget` now selects it: team size 1, no team name, no teammates, the price for one person. Picking a size still switches to a team entry. This applies to every race with the option on; it is not a per-event setting. Events without the option are unchanged.
