| #2266 | HEROVIDEO.1 — the coach video keeps the top of the frame and asks for sound | 2026-10-31. On `/50plus` the hero box cropped the coach clip from the centre and cut the coach's head off. The campaign block gains two operator choices beside the upload: **Video framing** (`video_focus`: top, centre or bottom stays in view; default top) and **Sound** (`sound_default`: on or start muted; default on). Browsers refuse autoplay with sound before a gesture, so `CampaignHero` calls `play()` unmuted after mount and falls back to muted autoplay with the Tap for sound button when refused (the old behaviour); an unknown value reads as the default; both fields are client fields. `CampaignHero.test.jsx` pins the framing classes and the three sound paths. |
