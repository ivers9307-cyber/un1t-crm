# SaaS Master Services Agreement — skeleton (SAAS4-C4)

> **DRAFT SKELETON for solicitor completion.** The current public
> `/terms` is a gym-member document; this is the B2B agreement a tenant
> gym signs. Settled commercial inputs baked in (Richard, 2026-07-19).
> **[SOLICITOR]** marks clauses to be written or finalised by counsel.

1. **Parties & definitions.** Champ Fitness Ltd ("Provider") and the
   customer gym ("Customer"). Definitions: Platform, Location, Plan,
   Add-on, Allowance, Overage, Hard Cap, DPA.

2. **The service.** The gym-management platform (CRM, bookings,
   communications, AI assistant where enabled, reporting), provided per
   Location, with integrations the Customer connects under its own
   third-party terms (Glofox, Meta, Xero, …).

3. **Term, trial.** Initial term monthly per Location. **30-day trial,
   card up-front**; converts to paid automatically unless cancelled in
   the trial.

4. **Fees.** Per-Location tier price + module add-ons, with metered
   allowances for WhatsApp, email and AI usage; consumption beyond an
   allowance draws on the prepaid wallet / bills at the published
   per-unit overage rates. Prices and allowances per the published plan
   schedule; changes on 30 days' notice, effective next billing cycle.

5. **Customer obligations.** Lawful use; accuracy of member data;
   maintaining its own member terms and privacy notice (the platform
   template assists but the Customer is the controller); securing its
   staff accounts.

6. **Acceptable use.** No spam (marketing only to opted-in members —
   the platform enforces consent flags and the Customer must not
   circumvent them); no unlawful content; Meta/WhatsApp policy
   compliance for messaging channels.

7. **Suspension.** Provider may suspend for material breach or
   non-payment after notice **[SOLICITOR: cure periods]**. Optional
   customer-set hard caps pause AI/campaign features per the product's
   documented behaviour (not a breach).

8. **Data protection.** The DPA (annexed) is incorporated. Customer =
   controller, Provider = processor. Subprocessor register at
   `/legal/subprocessors`.

9. **IP.** Platform IP stays the Provider's; Customer data stays the
   Customer's; Customer grants the licence needed to operate the
   service.

10. **Warranties & disclaimers.** **[SOLICITOR]** Service provided with
    reasonable skill and care; no uptime SLA in v1 (status/health
    surfaces provided); disclaimers to the extent permitted.

11. **Liability.** **[SOLICITOR]** Cap structure (suggested: 12 months'
    fees), carve-outs (data protection? **[SOLICITOR]**), exclusion of
    indirect loss, insurance.

12. **Termination & offboarding.** Either party on 30 days' notice to
    the end of a billing period; effect of termination = the settled
    offboarding sequence: suspension → export window → **60-day**
    retention → deletion (per the DPA and
    `docs/runbooks/tenant-offboarding.md`).

13. **General.** **[SOLICITOR]** Assignment, force majeure, notices,
    entire agreement, variation. Governing law and jurisdiction:
    **Ireland**.
